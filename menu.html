Brewing Guide

  1. Brewing the perfect cup of tea involves selecting fresh water, warming your vessel, measuring the leaves correctly, and steeping for the right amount of time based on the tea variety
  2. For green teas, use water that is around 175°F (80°C) and steep for 2-3 minutes. For black teas, use boiling water and steep for 3-5 minutes. Herbal teas can be steeped in boiling water for 5-7 minutes
  3. Experiment with different steeping times and temperatures to find your preferred flavor profile. Remember, the quality of the tea leaves and water will greatly affect the taste of your brew
